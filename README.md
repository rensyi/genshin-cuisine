# Genshin Cuisine

A fan-made, non-commercial recipe book that turns the dishes of Teyvat into real meals you can cook at home. Each recipe adapts an in-game dish from Genshin Impact into a real-world version, swapping fantasy ingredients for ones found in an ordinary kitchen or grocery store.

The site currently has 21 recipes, three for each of the seven regions: Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, and Snezhnaya.

## Disclosures

- **Unofficial fan project.** Genshin Cuisine is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. Genshin Impact, its dish names, and its icons are property of HoYoverse. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse.
- **Dish icons.** The images in `assets/icons/` are in-game dish icons that belong to HoYoverse. They are used here only to identify the dishes.
- **In-game facts.** Each dish's region, rarity, category, and in-game ingredients come from its page on the [Genshin Impact Wiki](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Food/List).
- **Recipes are not professionally tested.** The real-world recipes are fan adaptations based on standard versions of each dish and common food-safety guidance. Nobody has test-cooked them from this text, so use your own judgment in the kitchen.
- **Allergen and dietary tags are a guide only.** They are based on the ingredient lists, and packaged products vary by brand. Always check labels yourself.
- **AI assistance.** The site's code and recipe text were written with the help of an AI coding agent, following the project brief in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Fictional social handles.** The "Connect With Us" names in the footer (The Steambird, Kamera Feed, Akasha Live, Yae Gazette) are in-universe flavor text, not real accounts.

## Features

- Browse recipes with search and filters for region, difficulty, and diet
- A recipe page for each dish, with a "Teyvat → Kitchen" table explaining every ingredient swap
- Servings scaler, metric/US unit toggle, and an ingredient checklist
- Cook Mode: one step at a time, full screen, with countdown timers
- Favorites saved in your browser
- Print-friendly recipe pages

Each recipe is labeled by how close it is to a real dish:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Faithful | The in-game dish closely matches a real dish. |
| Adapted | There is a clear real dish underneath, with some ingredients or methods substituted. |
| Reimagined | The dish has no real counterpart, so the recipe is a creative take on how it looks and tastes in-game. |
