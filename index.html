About This Fan Project

Teyvat's kitchens run on Sweet Flowers and Fowl. Yours does not, so every recipe here goes through the same three steps before it reaches your stove.

  1. 1. Find the real dish

    Most Teyvat dishes are based on a real one. Tricolor Dango, for example, is Japanese hanami dango. Every recipe names the real dish it is built on.

  2. 2. Translate the ingredients

    Every recipe has a "Teyvat → Kitchen" table that swaps each in-game ingredient for a real one and says why. Sweet Flower becomes honey because it is used as a sweetener; Jueyun Chili becomes dried Sichuan chilies.

  3. 3. Say how close it is

    Faithful
    The in-game dish closely matches a real dish.
    Adapted
    There is a clear real dish underneath, but some ingredients or methods are substituted.
    Reimagined
    The dish has no real counterpart, so the recipe is a creative take on how it looks and tastes in-game.

In-game ingredient names and regions come from the Genshin Impact Wiki. Quantities, times, and temperatures are written for a real kitchen and follow standard food-safety guidance.
